# Boop Chess — Privacy Policy

Boop Chess does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

- All game progress (player name, avatar, points, unlocked levels and styles)
  is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.
- The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no
  account system.
- No data is shared with anyone because no data is collected.

If online multiplayer rooms are added in a future version, moves will be
relayed between players; this policy will be updated before that ships.
